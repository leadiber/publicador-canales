Publicador canales

A private tool for managing two YouTube channels

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

Publicador canales is a small private tool used by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels, Brass & Ruin and Bricked.

It runs on the owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to update the details of videos he has uploaded (title, description, thumbnail, captions, playlist, scheduled publishing time), to post comments as the channel, and to read his own channels' statistics for a weekly report.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Contact: through the About page of either channel on YouTube.
