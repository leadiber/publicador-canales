Who uses this app

Publicador canales is a private tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner's YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. It reads and updates the owner's own video details (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlist, privacy and scheduled publishing time), posts comments as the owner's channel, and reads the owner's channel statistics (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish and manage the owner's videos and to produce a private weekly report for the owner. Authorization tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer and storage drive. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact
